# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, accountability, and focus on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, personal or political attacks, trolling, publication of private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer contact configured in the repository’s GitHub security or community settings. Maintainers will review reports promptly, respect the reporter’s privacy, and apply responses proportionate to the circumstances, including correction, warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent removal.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project team.
